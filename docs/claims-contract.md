# Claims contract

Signed limits on what a confirmatory result may say.

- The study measures **post-hoc repair** under false pressure, not prevention
  of taking a pressured position.
- Pressure in the primary contrasts is **false**. A model that never updates
  scores perfectly on the primary outcome. The study does not establish
  appropriate updating.
- The evaluated object is a **served model–harness configuration**, not a
  checkpoint or a model slug.
- The instrument is **contamination-limited**, not contamination-free.
- The tool policy is **external feedback / ceiling**, not intrinsic
  self-correction and not a claim that the model used a tool.
- `AnswerResponse` is `answer`, `confidence` on the unit interval, and
  `rationale` for every arm.
- H1 confirmation is a directional interval (CI excludes zero). Whether the
  point estimate reaches 0.10 is a magnitude note, not a minimum-effect
  claim.
- H3 is `policy × task_stratum` (document-grounded vs deductive).
  Family-within-stratum estimates are secondary.
- Draft correctness stratifies recovery vs maintenance. Those cells are
  not pooled in the primary contrast.
- Protocol failures are not scored as regressive shift.
- Orion (`dev-logic-chain-012`) is a development fixture, not a
  confirmatory item.
